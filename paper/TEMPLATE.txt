ACL 2023 LaTeX template, as requested by the ULM course handout.
Source: https://2023.aclweb.org/downloads/acl2023.zip
Downloaded: 22 October 2026.

acl2023.sty and acl_natbib.bst are unmodified template files.
Their authorship and terms remain with the original authors.
The large example bibliography and template PDF are not included.

Compile main.tex with pdfLaTeX and BibTeX (or latexmk -pdf).
The course has not stated a page limit in the checked materials.
