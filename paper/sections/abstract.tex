\begin{itemize}
\item Main question: whose behavior does Centaur predict best, and what changes when we fine-tune on different populations?
\item Three datasets: the giving and taking variants of social allocation, categorization, and risky choice. Fine-tuning on US responses, Chinese responses, or an equal mix of both.
\item Main finding: who we fine-tune on changes the predictions. Better predictive performance and a good match to human differences on a new task variant don't always follow.
\end{itemize}
