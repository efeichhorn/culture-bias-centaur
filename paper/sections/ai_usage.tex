\section*{Declaration of AI Usage}

OpenAI Codex assisted with experiment code, data and output inspection, analyses, figures, literature retrieval, and manuscript drafting. Numerical statements in this working draft come from saved analysis outputs. Independent human verification of the code, data mappings, results, references, and wording remains necessary before submission. Pending analyses are identified in the text. AI systems are not authors and do not bear responsibility for the work.
